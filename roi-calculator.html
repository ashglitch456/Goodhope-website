How this is calculated

  • The Tire Retread & Repair Information Bureau (TRIB) states that a retreaded tire "sells for less — usually between 30 and 50 percent of the comparable new tire price." This tool applies that 30–50% band to the new-tire price you enter.
  • Cost per retread = your new-tire price × 30% to 50%.
  • Saving per tire = your new-tire price − the estimated retread cost (i.e. 50% to 70% of the new-tire price).
  • Per month = saving per tire × the number of tires you enter. Per year = per month × 12.
  • If you enter your own current retread cost, the second panel compares that figure directly to your new-tire price instead of the industry band.
  • Non-CAD currencies are shown with fixed approximate rates (1 CAD ≈ 0.73 USD ≈ 0.67 EUR ≈ 0.57 GBP), for illustration only, as of September 2026. All figures in this calculator stay in the currency you pick — no conversion is applied to the savings math.

Source: Tire Retread & Repair Information Bureau, "Learn More" — retread.org/learn-more (accessed September 2026). The 30–50% range is an industry-standard figure, not a Good Hope-specific offer.
