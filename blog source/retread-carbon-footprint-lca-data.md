## Sources used (fact-check pass)
1. Best Foot Forward LCA study (commissioned by the UK Centre for Remanufacturing and
   Reuse), 2009 — 17.5" light commercial vehicle tire: 86.9 kg CO2 (new) vs. 60.5 kg CO2
   (retread), a 30% reduction. Reported via Tyrepress
   (https://www.tyrepress.com/2009/06/retreads-deliver-30-lower-carbon-footprint-study/)
   and Tire Review Magazine
   (https://www.tirereview.com/retreads-deliver-30-lower-carbon-footprint-study/).
   Flagged in-article as an older study scoped to LCV tires, not truck tires.
2. Marangoni / University of Trento (Department of Industrial Engineering) LCA, 2024 —
   385/65R22.5 truck tire retreaded with RINGTREAD BLACKLINE WSS 300 M vs. new tire of
   the same size. ISO 14040/14044 methodology, tire PCR (UL10006, ISO 14025), SimaPro
   9.4 software. Result: 74% less CO2, 77% less resource consumption, better across all
   11 impact categories assessed. Reported via Tyrepress
   (https://www.tyrepress.com/2024/03/lca-shows-significant-advantages-for-marangoni-ringtread/),
   Retreading Business
   (https://www.retreadingbusiness.com/marangoni-lca-shows-74-less-co2-emissions/),
   Marangoni's own release
   (https://www.marangoni.com/with-marangoni-ringtread-tyres-74-less-co2-emissions-77-less-resource-consumption/),
   and Aggregates Business
   (https://www.aggbusiness.com/marangoni-ringtread-tyres-mean-74-less-co2-emissions).
   Flagged in-article as a manufacturer-commissioned LCA of one specific product line,
   not a generic industry average, and noted the study's rolling-resistance-parity
   assumption explicitly.
3. Scrap Tire News (2026), "Closing The Loop: Tire Retreading and the Circular Economy"
   (https://scraptirenews.com/2026/03/05/closing-the-loop-tire-retreading-and-the-circular-economy/)
   — casing represents ~75% of a commercial truck tire's total mass and a higher share
   of embodied energy/carbon; used to explain the mechanism behind both LCA results.
