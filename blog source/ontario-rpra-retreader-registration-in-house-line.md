# Ontario In-House Retread Line: Do You Need to Register With RPRA?

Summary: O. Reg. 225/18 defines a retreader by activity (replacing tread on worn tires). RPRA says retreaders must
register in the Registry (within 30 days of retreading in Ontario) and file an annual performance report (due May 31).
Weight categories merged to large (>700 kg) / other. Compliance Bulletin No. 9: track tires/records; buffings weight not
counted separately toward producer target. Unresolved: whether an own-fleet-only line must register — advise written
inquiry to RPRA. Source limits: RPRA/e-Laws pages not directly fetchable; relied on search-returned text.
