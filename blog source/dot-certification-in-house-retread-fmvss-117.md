## Sources (fact-checked this run, 2026-09-11)
1. **49 CFR 571.117 (FMVSS No. 117, Retreaded pneumatic tires)** — eCFR (ecfr.gov). Section 6.1 requires the retreader to affix its own DOT symbol certifying compliance. Primary US federal regulation.
2. **49 CFR 574.5 — Tire identification requirements** — eCFR / Cornell LII (law.cornell.edu). TIN structure, retreader plant-code-first format, symbol restrictions, 24-hour laser-etch window after mold removal. Primary US federal regulation.
3. **49 CFR 574.6 — How to obtain a plant code** — eCFR (ecfr.gov). Written application to NHTSA's Office of Vehicle Safety Compliance; separate code per plant. Primary US federal regulation.
4. **49 CFR Part 574 title/scope ("Tire Identification and Recordkeeping")** — eCFR (ecfr.gov). Basis for framing recordkeeping as a compliance obligation, not just a marking formality.
