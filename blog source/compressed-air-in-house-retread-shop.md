# Compressed Air on a Retread Line: Pressure, Clean Dry Air and the Cost of Leaks

Published 2026-11-15. Pillar A (product/equipment education): shop utilities for a retread line.

Curing, envelope work, casing cleaning and air tools all run on shop air. Where a retread line uses compressed air, why clean dry air matters at the buffed casing, and what the US DOE says leaks cost.

Hero: Adobe Stock 79200952 (industrial air compressors on receiver tanks), free-tier license, cropped/resized to 1600x800 locally with ImageMagick. Canva was tried first and returned quota_exceeded.

Sources:
- Overdrive (overdriveonline.com), "One more round": precure plant example with 85 psi chamber pressure, 70 psi inside envelopes, 260°F, about a three-hour cure (via search extract).
- US Patent 8,632,647, "Method of controlling tread shrinkage during curing": chamber pressure of about 85–105 psia, curing temperature around 127°C (via search extract). Other precure patents (incl. US 4,203,793; EP 0399942): envelope under at least partial vacuum; envelope pressure held below chamber pressure (via search extracts).
- US Patent 9,044,909, "Tyre retreading method and system" (and related retreading patents): rubber dust from buffing and skiving blown off with oil-free, dry compressed air; visible surface moisture removed before tread application (via search extracts).
- Modern Tire Dealer, "'I thought I knew a lot about retreads'": buffed area must be kept clean and never touched; dirt and oils reduce adhesion of new tread (via search extract).
- US Department of Energy, Compressed Air Systems Fact Sheet #7, "Compressed Air System Leaks" (Rev. 1, 2026), hosted by the Compressed Air Challenge: leaks sometimes waste 20–30% of compressor output; typical poorly maintained plant around 20% leak rate (via search extract).
- US Department of Energy compressed air tip sheet: proactive leak detection and repair can reduce leaks to less than 10% of compressor output (via search extract).
- Natural Resources Canada, compressed air tip sheet (CIPEC): shut off compressed air when not required; check joints, valves, fittings and hose connections for leaks (via search extract).
- Save on Energy (IESO), Retrofit Program prescriptive and custom stream pages: compressed air measures incl. VSD compressor up to $34,280 and zero-loss drain; custom stream non-lighting incentive the greater of $1,800/kW or $0.20/kWh; "Retrofit program changes" notice with changes effective November 30, 2026 (via search extracts).
