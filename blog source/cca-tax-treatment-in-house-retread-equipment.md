SOURCES (all via WebSearch this run, Sept 19 2026 — see limitation note above):
1. Class 43 — 30% declining-balance CCA class for eligible M&P machinery/equipment not in
   Class 29 or 53; half-year rule normally applies to new additions. Canada.ca "Classes of
   depreciable property" page (appeared in search index); corroborated by t2inc.ca, Workday
   CCA explainer, gondaliyacpa.ca "Capital Cost Allowance Canada 2026" guide.
2. Class 53 — 50% declining-balance CCA class for M&P equipment acquired/available for use
   after 2015 and before 2026; after 2025, new M&P equipment additions go to Class 43
   instead. mehmigroup.com "CCA Class 53 Canada: 50% Rate for M&P Equipment"; corroborated
   by Densmore CPA "Technical Spotlight: CCA", Knowledge Bureau "Year End Planning: CCA Tax
   Breaks Phasing Out Soon", Fuller Landau LLP, Craig Ross LLP CCA update articles.
3. Accelerated Investment Incentive (AII) — suspends half-year rule for eligible property
   acquired after Nov 20, 2018; for M&P equipment (Class 53) specifically, enhanced
   first-year deduction was ~100% for property available for use before 2024, stepping down
   to 75% for 2024–2025 and 55% for 2026–2027, before reverting to standard treatment.
   Canada.ca "Accelerated investment incentive" page (appeared in search index); corroborated
   by Densmore CPA, Knowledge Bureau, Fuller Landau LLP, Craig Ross LLP (independent
   accounting-firm sources, consistent with each other on the phase-down schedule).
4. Half-year rule mechanics (50% of normal first-year CCA on net additions). Canada.ca CCA
   pages (T4002 guide, individual CCA page); taxtron.ca "The Half-Year Rule for Capital Cost
   Allowance"; KPU Pressbooks "Introductory Canadian Tax."
5. Old CCPC immediate-expensing measure: $1.5 million/year limit, shared among associated
   corporations, for property acquired after April 18, 2021; expired for CCPCs Dec 31, 2023,
   no longer available. BDO Canada "Immediate expensing of depreciable property" (per search
   synthesis); corroborated by Knowledge Bureau, NG Chartered Professional Accountants
   "The Evolution Of Immediate Expensing."
6. NEW — Productivity Mega Deduction: announced Sept 15, 2026 by the federal government
   (Department of Finance / PM Carney); draft legislative proposals for a PERMANENT,
   broad-based 100% immediate-expensing regime for eligible depreciable property acquired
   and available for use after September 14, 2026, including manufacturing/processing
   machinery and equipment; expands share of eligible business capital investment from
   roughly 15% to over 65%; estimated fiscal cost ~$36 billion over 5 years starting
   2026–27. NOT YET ENACTED LAW as of this post's publish date — flagged explicitly in the
