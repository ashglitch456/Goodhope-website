# Is Agricultural Tire Retreading Worth It in 2026?

Pillar A — product/equipment/process education (market trend, genuinely new ground: no
existing post touches agricultural tires at all).

Published: 2026-09-15

## Angle

2026 farm economy pressure (NCGA survey via Tire Review: 58% of farmers may postpone
equipment purchases) is pushing farmers toward tire replacement over equipment
replacement. That naturally raises the question of whether retreading applies to ag
tires the way it does to commercial trucking. Honest answer: it depends which tire.
Large row-crop tractor drive tires — no major branded retread program found. LT-cased
utility/implement tires and large OTR-scale ag equipment — real, established retread
supply chains.
