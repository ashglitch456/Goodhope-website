# Paying for Retread Technician Training in Ontario: What the Ontario Job Grant Covers

Published 2026-11-15. Pillar C (in-house retreading for fleet operators): funding the training side of an in-house line.

Running your own retread line means building your own bench of skilled technicians. What Ontario's redesigned job grant pays for, who has to deliver the training, and what it won't cover.

Sources:
- Ontario.ca, "Ontario Job Grant" (old Canada-Ontario Job Grant page now redirects there): general maximum of $10,000 per trainee; training must be delivered by an approved third-party provider (via search extracts).
- Ontario Ministry, "Ontario Job Grant Application Guidelines, effective May 1, 2026" (forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca): up to $10,000 ministry contribution per trainee; up to $15,000 for employers with fewer than 100 employees training previously unemployed new hires; up to $500 per trainee for materials/textbooks/software; up to $500 travel only where distance qualifies; unlisted costs ineligible (via search extracts; full PDF could not be opened).
- Third-party summaries of the 2026 guidelines (govmoney.ca, northgrants.ca, learnsignal.com, hellodarwin.com, grantcompass.ca): COJG paused November 2025 and relaunched as OJG; employers with 100+ employees contribute at least 50%; eligible provider types; Ontario presence and two years offering the training; training in Ontario, named trainees, completed within 52 weeks; apprenticeship training ineligible; trainee wages and pre-agreement costs ineligible (via search extracts; secondary, marked as such in the post).
- Tire Industry Association, Commercial Tire Service training page (tireindustry.org): Basic CTS 200-level for new hires; Certified CTS for employees with at least two years' hands-on experience (via search extract).
- Tire Review: Ontario Tire Dealers Association offers TIA training programs in Ontario (via search extract).
- The 'why this matters' section and the note on supplier setup guidance are labelled in the post as our own reasoning.
