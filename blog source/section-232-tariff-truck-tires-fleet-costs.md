# The Section 232 Truck Parts Tariff: What a 25% Duty on Imported Tires Means for Fleet Costs in 2026

Pillar: B (Economics/authority — regulatory/trade)
Published: 2026-09-17

## Premise
A U.S. Section 232 tariff on medium/heavy-duty trucks, buses, and truck parts (explicitly
including tires) took effect November 1, 2025 and has been reshaping replacement-tire
economics through 2026. This is distinct from the existing `canada-china-truck-tire-
dumping-investigation-2026.html` post, which covers a separate Canadian CBSA anti-dumping/
subsidy case opened August 31, 2026. This post covers the U.S. Section 232 action: what's
taxed, the USMCA treatment, the offset program, and how it sharpens (without replacing)
the existing retread cost-per-mile case. Audience: primarily U.S. fleet operators buying
replacement tires; noted as distinct from the Canadian trade story already covered.

## Sources (verified via web search, September 17, 2026)
1. Legal basis and headline rates — 25% tariff on medium/heavy-duty trucks (Classes III-VIII)
   and truck parts, 10% tariff on buses, invoked under Section 232 of the Trade Expansion
   Act of 1962 on national-security grounds — RVIA, "President Trump Issues Section 232
   Tariffs: 25% On Medium- And Heavy-Duty Vehicles, 10% On Buses Starting November 1"
   (rvia.org/news-insights/president-trump-issues-section-232-tariffs-25-medium-and-heavy-duty-vehicles-10-buses-starting-november-1)
2. Effective date (12:01 a.m. EDT, November 1, 2025) and explicit confirmation that "tires"
   are named among covered parts (engines, transmissions, tires, chassis) — KPMG, "US
   imposes tariffs on medium/heavy duty vehicle imports" (kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/us-imposes-tariffs-medium-heavy-duty-vehicle-imports.html);
   corroborated by C.H. Robinson client advisory (chrobinson.com) and GHY International
   trade-compliance note (ghy.com/trade-compliance/section-232-tariffs-on-heavy-medium-duty-trucks-and-buses-effective-nov-1)
3. USMCA treatment — for USMCA-eligible trucks/parts, the tariff applies only to non-U.S.
   content value; but USMCA-eligible goods currently remain duty-free under this action
   until Commerce/CBP finalize a process for identifying and applying tariffs to non-U.S.
   content — Butzel Long, "New Section 232 Tariffs on Trucks, Truck Parts, and Buses, Take
   Effect November 1" (butzel.com); corroborated by KPMG
4. Import Adjustment Offset Program — vehicle manufacturers assembling in the U.S. can
   qualify for an offset equal to 3.75% of the aggregate value of U.S.-assembled vehicles,
   Nov. 1, 2025 through Oct. 31, 2030 — BDO, "New Section 232 Tariffs on Trucks and Buses
   Take Effect; Offset Program Extended Through 2030" (bdo.com/insights/tax/new-section-232-tariffs-on-trucks-and-buses-take-effect-offset-program-extended-through-2030)
5. Aftermarket/replacement-tire cost impact framing — Fleet Equipment Magazine and
   aftermarketNews, "What a 25% Tariff on Truck Parts Means for the Aftermarket"
   (fleetequipmentmag.com/tariff-on-truck-parts-section-232; aftermarketnews.com/tariffs-truck-parts-section-232)
6. Recurring Commerce Dept. process (starting 2026) that can add further automotive/truck
   parts to the Section 232 list, difficult to remove once added — Automotive Body Parts
   Association, "Section 232 Auto Parts Tariff Inclusion Process Begins in 2026"
   (autobpa.com/2025/12/23/section-232-auto-parts-tariff-inclusion-process-begins-in-2026)
7. General national-security rationale and market-impact framing — S&P Global Mobility,
   "New Section 232 tariffs target truck, bus imports" (spglobal.com/automotive-insights)
