## Sources used (verified 2026-08-31 via WebSearch; WebFetch blocked by
session egress policy on all reference domains)

- Skyliner Truck Center, "Truck Tire Costs in 2026: New, Retread, and Recap
  Pricing" — new tire $300-800 by position, retread $150-400, 40-60% savings,
  80-90% comparable mileage.
- General industry range (retread = 30-50% of new tire cost) — cross-checked
  against multiple current sources; consistent with the figure already used
  in `retreads-vs-new-tires-cost-per-mile.html`.
- AcuTread, "How Many Times Can Your Tires Be Retreaded?" — long-haul casings
  ~2-3 cycles, short-haul/regional ~5-6 cycles; Michelin casing warranty
  covers up to 3 retreads.
- 2018 Bandag/Bridgestone-sponsored fleet study (via Heavy Duty
  Trucking/Trucking Info) — retread adoption by fleet size: <5 trucks 34%,
  5-24 trucks 55%, 25-99 trucks ~two-thirds, 100+ trucks 87%.
- TRIB (via Heavy Duty Trucking/Trucking Info) — 89% of fleets with 500+
  trucks use retreads.
- This site's own `cost-to-set-up-tire-retread-shop.html` — $45,000-$62,000
  CAD starter equipment/tooling range (cited as our own prior published
  analysis, not new external data).
