# Retreads on the Steer Axle: Why Canada's Rule Is Stricter Than the US's

Pillar: B — Economics/authority (regulatory contrast, Ontario/cross-border relevance)
Published: 2026-09-04

## Angle
"No retreads on the steer axle" is often repeated as if it's one universal rule. It
isn't — Canada's National Safety Code bans retreads on any steering axle for any
vehicle, while US federal rules (49 CFR 393.75) restrict only buses. Good Hope is
in Windsor, ON, right at the Ontario–Michigan border, so a regulatory-gap piece aimed
at fleets/shops that operate across or near that line is directly relevant and not
previously covered — the existing safety post (retread-tire-safety-trib-tia-data.html)
is about the federal debris study and DOT certification generally, not steer-axle
placement rules specifically.

## Sources (checked live via web search, 2026-09-04)
1. **CCMTA National Safety Code Standard 11 — Maintenance and Periodic Inspection
   (January 2020).** Official PDF:
   https://www.ccmta.ca/web/default/files/PDF/NSC-English/CCMTA%20NSC%20Standard%2011%20-%20January%202020%20-%20ENG.pdf
   Confirmed via two independent web searches (search-tool synthesis of the document,
   cross-checked against a provincial mirror at novascotia.ca): "No retreaded tire
   shall be installed and operated on an active steering axle." Also: no retread
   material shall be loose, missing, or separated at the bond interface.
   Note: direct WebFetch of the PDF was blocked by this session's network egress
   policy (as was every other regulatory-document domain tried) — sourced instead
   from the search tool's synthesis of the official document, corroborated across
   two independently-worded queries returning the same clause. A human reviewer
   with normal web access can verify the PDF directly at the link above.
2. **49 CFR § 393.75(d)-(e) — Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations.**
   (d): no bus shall be operated with regrooved, recapped, or retreaded tires on
   front wheels. (e): regrooved tires ≥4,920 lbs load rating restricted on front
   wheels of trucks/truck-tractors — a narrower, separate restriction from retreads
   generally. Confirmed via web search summarizing FMCSA text (cross-referenced via
   https://blog.gwccnet.com/blog/ask-a-safety-rep-can-i-use-retreaded-tires-on-the-steer-axle
   and general trade-press coverage of the "can retreads go on steer axles" question,
   e.g. https://www.fleetmaintenance.com/equipment/brakes-tire-and-wheel/article/21082955/should-retreads-be-used-as-steer-tires).
3. **CVSA out-of-service tread depth criteria.** Steer minimum 4/32", other positions
   2/32"; OOS if steer tire is below 2/32" in two adjacent grooves. Via
   https://www.fleetowner.com/equipment/article/21682772/fmcsa-cvsa-and-tread-depth.
