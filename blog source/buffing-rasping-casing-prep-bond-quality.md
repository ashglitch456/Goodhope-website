# Buffing & Rasping: The Casing-Prep Step That Determines Retread Bond Quality

Pillar: A — product/equipment education
Published: 2026-08-31
Live post: `blog/buffing-rasping-casing-prep-bond-quality.html`

## Angle

Buffing/rasping (casing surface prep before tread application) had no
existing post despite dedicated product pages (buffing-machine.html,
buffing-blade.html, cone-rasps.html, curved-cup-rasps.html,
radius-cup-contour-rasps.html, radius-faced-circular-rasps.html,
rasp-assembly.html). Angle: buff texture, not just tread choice or cure,
determines bond quality — and under-/over-buffing are the two failure
modes that ruin a casing.

## Sources used (verified 2026-08-31 via WebSearch; WebFetch blocked by
session egress policy on all reference domains attempted: retread.org,
tirereview.com, tireindustry.org, truckinginfo.com, acutread.com)

- TIA/TRMG RP-01/02-23, "BTS6 — Standard Buffing Textures for Tire
  Retreading and Repairing" — six molded reference texture classifications;
  jointly published/maintained by TRMG with TRIB and TIA.
- TIA/TRMG RP-01-01, "Tire Profiling for Retreading" (updated 11/2019) —
  buffing to correct radius/contour for precure vs. mold-cure tread.
- USPTO tire-buffing-apparatus and tire-rasp-blade patent literature
  (general engineering description) — buffing purpose (surface area/
  adhesion, oxidized-rubber removal), radial/axial blade tooth
  arrangement, under-buff vs. over-buff failure modes (tread separation
  vs. steel-belt exposure), automated undertread-measurement systems.
- Future Market Insights, "Tire Retreading Equipment Market" report —
  global equipment market ~$547.0M (2025) to ~$899.6M (2035), 5.1% CAGR.

No specific undertread-depth (mm) spec is stated in the post — no public,
citable number was found this run, so the post describes the general
principle (avoid exposing steel belts) rather than inventing a figure.
