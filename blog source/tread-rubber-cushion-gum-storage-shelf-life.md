# Tread Rubber and Cushion Gum Storage: The Rules That Actually Protect Bond Quality

Pillar: A (product/equipment/consumables education)
Published: 2026-09-09

## Angle
Consumables shelf life and storage is an underserved, purely practical topic — no existing
post on the blog covers it. Ties directly to bond-quality themes already covered in the
buffing/rasping and curing-envelope posts.

## Sources used (found via web search this run)
- Tire Industry Association — Tread Rubber and Tire Repair Materials Manufacturers' Group
  (TRMG), TRMG-1 publication (tireindustry.org): store dry, out of sunlight, below 80°F;
  ~6-month satisfactory shelf life under those conditions from date of receipt; 80–95°F
  cuts shelf life ~25%; 95–110°F cuts it ~50%+; colder storage extends shelf life without
  harm. This is the spine of the post — a primary industry-association source.
- REMA TIP TOP — OTR retread precure cushion gum product page: 4-month shelf life from
  date of manufacture (used to show manufacturer specs can run tighter than the general
  TRMG baseline).
- Patch Rubber Co. — precure cushion gum product page: some products carry a 3-month
  shelf life (used the same way — tighter-than-baseline example).
