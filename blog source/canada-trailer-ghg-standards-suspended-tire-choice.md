## Angle
Oct 13, 2026 interim order (Canada Gazette I, Oct 31, 2026) continues suspension of trailer GHG standards (never in force since 2019). Those standards included LRR tires + TPMS/ATIS. Reason: U.S. alignment after D.C. Cir. vacated EPA trailer rules (2021). Result: trailer tire choices stay economic; LRR/SmartWay retreads and inflation discipline still pay; in-house line lets a fleet pick its own trailer tread.
