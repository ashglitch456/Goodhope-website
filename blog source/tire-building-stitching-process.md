# Tire Building & Stitching: How the Tread Actually Gets Bonded to the Casing

Pillar: A — Product/equipment education

## Angle
Between buffing/casing prep and curing sits an under-covered step: the tire builder,
where cushion gum and precured tread are applied to the casing and stitched down
before the assembly goes into the envelope and curing chamber. No existing post
covers building/stitching/splicing as its own topic (verified via grep across
blog/*.html — only a one-sentence mention in tire-retreading-equipment-buyers-guide.html).
Ties directly to the tire-builder.html and expandable-hub.html product pages.

## Sources used (fact-check pass)
1. TRIB (Tire Retread & Repair Information Bureau) / TIA precure process overview —
   retread.org, "How a Tire is Retreaded" (https://www.retread.org/copy-of-join-trib) —
   used for the overall process sequence (inspect → buff → skive/repair → build/cure).
2. Tire Review Magazine coverage of TRIB's process/buyer's guide —
   https://www.tirereview.com/retreading-tires-guide-trib/ — corroborates the step
   sequence and that TRIB publishes an official step-by-step guide.
3. U.S. Patent 4,004,961, "Stitching apparatus for stitching tire components on a
   building drum" — primary technical source for the definition of "stitching" as
   expulsion of trapped air via rollers.
4. U.S. Patent 4,075,047, "Tire retreading system" (Bandag Incorporated) — describes
   the building-station mechanics (rotating column, expandable hub, tread application
   and air-removal on opposite sides).
5. U.S. Patent 4,234,371, "Method and apparatus for applying a circumferential tread
   to a tire carcass" — circumferential tread application / splice mechanics.
6. U.S. Patent 6,758,931, "Method and apparatus for applying tire tread for a retread
   tire" — corroborating splice/tread-application description.
7. U.S. Patent 5,447,587, "Method for applying hot cushion gum to a tire carcass" —
   cushion gum application method.
8. U.S. Patent 5,458,727, "Direct extrusion cushion-gumming and skive-filling of tire
   casings for retreading" — corroborating cushion gum application description.

All patent citations used only to describe established, decades-documented mechanical
process (what stitching/splicing/cushion-gum application are and why they're done),
not to make any proprietary or company-specific claim.
